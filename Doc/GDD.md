_1. Game Overview_

### What is Lincoln's Labyrinthine?

The game titled Lincoln's Labyrinthine is a 2D puzzle adventure game about a cartoon bat that must navigate through a huge series of rooms in linear fashion.

One thing to note is that this game is not meant for children. It has some gorey and violent content, not really suited for children. However, it has a slight sense of humor.

### Who is the target audience?

Well first we need to discuss what platform this game is on. It will be on the Panic Playdate, which is a handheld console that has a crank on the side. The target audience is people who like puzzle games, and people who like to play games on the Playdate.

### The Start of the Story

Coming home from his job, Lincoln the Long Earred Bat struggles to keep his composure as he passes by his fellow neighbors. Some wave and some smile. One of them trims the hedge on their lawn, and the clippers make Lincoln worry. He seems to find danger everywhere he looks. He is a nervous bat, and he is always looking over his shoulder. He is always looking for danger, and he is always looking for a way out.

He runs home, and realizes that being home was not as safe as he thought. But he felt comfortable.

He sat on his couch, and he felt safe enough. Hours fly by, and his clock strikes midnight. Lincoln walks into his bedroom, its dark, and a little dusty. It was unkept. Clothes, food wrappers, and beer bottles laid on the floor. He lays his back against the wall and looks at the corner of his room, where a tilted frame shows a picture of Lasse. Lasse has not been around for months. He thought he filed a police report, but ever since that day... his memory has gotten pretty hazy.

Images of laughter of him and his betrothed outside his porch looking at fireworks flash by. He picked up one of the beer bottles and throws it against the wall.

"Why is this happening to me??" he asks. He grabs his head. More flashing images of Lasse looking back at him. He begins to breathe heavily. Waddling across the room, losing his balance, he looks up, and it looks as if all the walls around him are beginning to expand.

He puts his hands down. Looking around, he asks again, "What is happening to me??"

He screams, again. "What is happening to me??"

He stumbles. He sees a glass of water by his desk. He takes a sip, and he hears moments of laughter, and the room begins to shrink.

Opening his eyes, he realizes that something is wrong. "No..." he continues, "Something is seriously wrong."

He goes to dial 911, but all that plays from the phone is only laughter. At first, it sounds... like the laugh he shared with Lasse. And as it continued, it became more and more unrecognizable.

"Whoever is doing this," he mutters, "Leave me alone. Get out of this house! Just leave me alone!"

Suddenly, a hand touches his shoulder. He jolts, looking behind him. Silence, and nothingness.

"Hello?" he asks, softly and nervously.

Then he clutches his fists, and asks again, aggressively. "Hello? Who is there?!"

A door opens behind him. The creaking, so it would seem, gave him the chills. The room no longer resembled anything normal. What he saw before him, was a skinny, long, narrow hallway. A dark, dark hallway. Walls too dark to see their detail. An old fashioned carpet painted in a pattern on the ground. The only thing that was audible, was the creaking of the door.

50 feet away, a light flickers above the door. Behind him was a wall, that was not there before. He turns around, and sees the wall. He looks back at the door, and it nearly... taunts him. Its presence is so strong, that it seems to be calling him. He takes a step forward, and the floor is creaking under his feet. He knows what he must do.

_4. Gameplay Mechanics_

### Controls
Easy to learn, easy to pick up.

These should be the controls for the game:
- Move Left: Left D-Pad (Run by double tapping)
- Move Right: Right D-Pad (Run by double tapping)
- Jump: A Button (Press again to double jump)
- Attack/Interact: B Button
- Crouch: Down D-Pad (Double tap to fall through platforms)
- Pause: Menu Button

### Meters
There will be a few meters in the game, and they ALL have a purpose.

#### Health Meter
This ones self explanatory.

The health meter will be out of 100. The player will start with 100 health, and they will lose health when they take damage from enemies, traps, or other hazards. The player can regain health by finding health pickups, or by using a health potion.

#### Stamina Meter
The stamina meter will be out of 100. The player will start with 100 stamina, and they will lose stamina when they run, jump, or attack. The player can regain stamina by standing still, walking, or using a stamina potion. If the player runs out of stamina, they will be unable to run, jump, or attack until they regain some stamina.

#### Sanity Meter
This ones important. The sanity meter will also be out of 100, however it is heavily attached to the story, the players actions, and the players choices. The player will start with 50 sanity. The player will lose sanity when they take damage from enemies, traps, or other hazards. The player can regain sanity by finding sanity pickups, or by using a sanity potion. If the player runs out of sanity, they will be unable to see the world around them clearly, and have hallucinations. Other effects may also occur, but we will not go into detail about that yet. The sanity meter is important to the story, and the players choices will affect the story, and the players sanity.

_5. Level Design_
---
### Level Design Philosophy
There are a few rules that need to happen before we even consider starting to make levels.
1. The player should be able to get to the end of a level without dying. 
2. The player should be able to know what to do next, and where to go next.
3. The player should have no trouble understanding the obstacles, and the puzzles in the level.
4. The level makers should have everything for that area before starting on a level made for that area.
5. The level makers should have a clear understanding of the story, the characters, the controls, and the mechanics of the game before starting on a level made for that area.
6. A vision of the area should be clear before starting a level.
7. Any player should be able to play the level, and understand the level just by looking at it. 

### Level Design Goals
One challenge we faced was the quantity of the levels. How much do we wanna make?

I, DiSKOdev, claim that I could make 1000 levels within 24 hours. However, I do not feel like this is necessary YET. So we will start with 255. A simple number, but a good number. We will start with 255 levels, and we will see how that goes. If we feel like we need more levels, we can always add more levels later.

I have once tried to make a level generator in Python. However without the proper rules being set, it was not possible to make a level generator that would make levels that were fun, and challenging. So we will have to make the levels by hand.

Thus, the goal is simple:

##### Make 255 levels, and make them fun, challenging, and rewarding. 

One thing that needs to happen is that we need to make a roadmap for each level being made. This roadmap will help Settings into segments, where each segment is a bunch of levels of a similar mode.

What are these modes?

#### Mode 1: Puzzle Mode
Simply put, the player will have to solve puzzles to get through the level. The puzzles will be simple at first, and they will get more complex as the player progresses through the levels. The puzzles will be designed to be fun, challenging, and rewarding.

#### Mode 2: Chase Mode
In this mode, the player will have to run away from an instance. The levels will be designed to create a sense of urgency and excitement, challenging the player's reflexes and decision-making skills.

#### Mode 3: Stealth Mode
In this mode, the player will have to avoid being seen by enemies. The levels will be designed to create a sense of tension and suspense, challenging the player's stealth and strategy skills.

#### Mode 4: Scenic Mode
This mode is a breather from the intense gameplay of the other modes. The player will explore the environment, enjoy the visuals, and uncover story elements. The levels will be designed to provide a relaxing and immersive experience.

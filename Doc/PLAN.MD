## Overview
This goes into the overall plan for Lincoln's Labyrinthine, including the story, gameplay mechanics, and development roadmap. This document will be updated as the project progresses.

## Story
Lincoln is a young cartoon crow who after a long days of work, decides to take a break on his couch in his own home. After a while, the clock strikes midnight, and Lincoln is ready to head to bed. His mindset has been broken for quite some time, as he has been getting nightmares and has been trouble sleeping, especially after the loss of his fiancee. However, tonight is different. He suddenly sees his storage room door open, and he hears a voice calling him. He goes to investigate, and finds himself trapped in a labyrinthine maze. His own house has been transformed into a maze, and he must navigate through it to find his way out. Along the way, he will encounter various obstacles and enemies, as well as clues about his past and the nature of the maze.

## Audience
This game in particular is for those who own a Playdate console, and are looking for a horror game to play on it. There is no official age rating for the game, but it is recommended for players aged 13 and up due to the horror themes and imagery.

## Gameplay Mechanics
### Controls
- D-pad: Move Lincoln left or right using the left or right D-pad buttons. You can double tap the D-pad to run in that direction.
- A Button: Jump. Pressing the A button while in the air will allow Lincoln to double jump.
- B Button: Interact. Pressing the B button will allow Lincoln to interact with certain objects in the environment, such as levers or doors. You can also attack smaller enemies by pressing the B button when close to them.
- Crank: The crank will be used for certain puzzles and mechanics throughout the game. For example, there may be a puzzle where you need to crank a mechanism to open a door, or a mechanic where you need to crank to generate power for certain areas.

### Enemies and Obstacles
The game will feature a variety of enemies and obstacles that players will need to overcome. These may include:
- Basic enemies: These are the most common enemies in the game, and will come in various forms such as rats, spiders, and ghosts. They will have simple attack patterns and can be defeated with a few hits.
- Bosses: These are more powerful enemies that will require more strategy and skill to defeat. They will have unique attack patterns and may require players to use specific mechanics or
- Instances: The real meat and potatoes of what makes the game unique. These special enemies are ones that cannot be defeated through normal means, and instead require the player to solve a puzzle or complete a specific task to get past them. For example, there may be an instance where the player needs to find a hidden switch to disable a trap, or an instance where the player needs to use the crank to generate power to open a door. Worse, you may as well run for your life, as some of these instances may be deadly if you don't solve them in time.

### Puzzles
The game will feature a variety of puzzles that players will need to solve to progress. These may include:
- Environmental puzzles: These are puzzles that require players to interact with the environment in specific ways to progress. For example, there may be a puzzle where players need to move boxes to reach a higher platform or activate switches in a certain order.
- Crank puzzles: These are puzzles that require players to use the crank to progress. For example, there may be a puzzle where players need to crank a mechanism to open a door or generate power for certain areas.
- Logic puzzles: These are puzzles that require players to use logic and critical thinking to solve. For example, there may be a puzzle where players need to decipher a code or figure out a pattern to progress.

## Level Design
The level design will be focused on creating a sense of tension and unease for the player. There is going to be seven main sections of the game, each with its own unique theme and set of challenges. The sections will be as follows:
1. The Mansion - The first section of the game will take place in Lincoln's mansion, which has been transformed into a maze. This section will serve as an introduction to the game's mechanics and story, and will feature relatively simple puzzles and enemies.
2. The Caves - The second section of the game will take place in a series of underground caves. This section will be more challenging than the first, with more complex puzzles and tougher enemies.
3. The Laboratory - The third section of the game will take place in a laboratory, where players will encounter more advanced technology and machinery. This section will feature more complex puzzles that require the use of the crank mechanic.
4. The Circus - The fourth section of the game will take place in a creepy circus, with a variety of different attractions and performers. This section will feature more surreal and unsettling imagery, as well as more challenging puzzles and enemies.
5. The Factory - The fifth section of the game will take place in a factory, where players will encounter more industrial machinery and hazards. This section will feature more complex puzzles that require precise timing and coordination.
6. The Hospital - The sixth section of the game will take place in a hospital, where players will encounter more medical equipment and unsettling imagery. This section will feature more challenging puzzles that require players to think creatively and use their environment to their advantage.
7. The Asylum - The final section of the game will take place in an asylum, where players will encounter the most disturbing and unsettling imagery in the game. This section will feature the toughest puzzles and enemies, and will serve as the climax of the game's story.

The game is currently planned to feature a total of 255 rooms spread across its seven main sections. Each room will offer a unique combination of layouts, puzzles, obstacles, and enemies to keep exploration engaging throughout the adventure. Progression is primarily linear, requiring players to complete one room before advancing to the next. However, optional rooms and hidden areas will reward curious players with useful items, upgrades, and secrets. As players progress, they will also unlock the ability to revisit previously completed rooms or warp to later areas after obtaining specific items or meeting certain conditions.

## Items
The game will feature a variety of items that players can collect and use to help them progress through the game. Some examples may include:
- Health items: These items will restore Lincoln's health when collected. They may come in the form of food, potions, or other consumables.
- Keys: These items will be used to unlock doors and progress through the game. They may be hidden in the environment or dropped by enemies.
- Flashlight: This item will allow Lincoln to see in dark areas and reveal hidden objects or paths.
- Sanity Pill: This item will temporarily boost Lincoln's sanity, allowing him to withstand more encounters with disturbing imagery and enemies without losing health.
- Hatchet: This item will allow Lincoln to attack enemies and break certain objects in the environment. It may be found in certain rooms.

## Oh yeah, the Sanity Mechanic
The sanity mechanic is a core part of the gameplay, and will add an extra layer of challenge and tension to the game. As Lincoln encounters disturbing imagery and enemies, his sanity will decrease. If his sanity reaches zero, he will lose health instead. However, players can use the Sanity Pill item to temporarily boost Lincoln's sanity and withstand more encounters without losing health. The sanity mechanic will encourage players to be strategic about how they approach certain situations, and will add an extra layer of immersion to the horror experience.

If your sanity gets too low, you may start to see hallucinations and experience more intense encounters with enemies and disturbing imagery. This will make it even more important to manage your sanity and use the Sanity Pill item wisely.

## Instances
These are the enemies that *you as the player cannot defeat through normal means*. Instead, you will need to solve a puzzle or complete a specific task to get past them. For example, there may be an instance where you need to find a hidden switch to disable a trap, or an instance where you need to use the crank to generate power to open a door. Some of these instances may be deadly if you don't solve them in time, so be prepared to think quickly and act decisively.

So what are some of these instances?

### The Shadow Man
This enemy slowly stalks you through the rooms, appearing in the shadows, and will attempt to grab you if you are not careful. If he catches you, he will drain your sanity and health, making it even more important to manage your resources and stay alert.
#### What To Do
Get out of there! The Shadow Man is not to be fought, but rather avoided. He may block off certain paths in your ways, so you will need to find alternative routes to progress. Keep an eye out for clues and hints in the environment that may help you avoid him.

### The Mimic
A creature that can take the form of objects in the environment, such as doors, boxes, or even other enemies. The Mimic will attempt to trick you into interacting with it, and if you do, it will attack you and drain your sanity and health.
#### What To Do
Be cautious and observant. Look for subtle differences in the environment that may indicate the presence of the Mimic. For example, a door that looks slightly different from the others may be a Mimic in disguise. Use your wits and intuition to avoid falling into its trap.

### So what is Lincoln's Labyrinthine?
This is a game about a crow who, after a long days of work, decides to take a break on his couch in his own home. After a while, the clock strikes midnight, and Lincoln is ready to head to bed. His mindset has been broken for quite some time, as he has been getting nightmares and has been trouble sleeping, especially after the loss of his fiancee. However, tonight is different. He suddenly sees his storage room door open, and he hears a voice calling him. He goes to investigate, and finds himself trapped in a labyrinthine maze. His own house has been transformed into an endless room maze, and he must navigate through it to find his way out. Along the way, he will encounter various obstacles and enemies, as well as clues about his past and the nature of the maze.

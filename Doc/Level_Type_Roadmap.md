# Level Type Roadmap

##### This is a roadmap that tells us which levels are gonna be for what purpose. 

## What are the Level Types?

There are a few level types that we will go over here.

### Explore Type

These types are simply just getting from point A to B. No to some danger is found here. 

### Chase Type

Dangerous instances lurk the halls here, and you must run from them.

### Puzzle Type

You must solve puzzles to get through these levels.

### Landmark Type

These are somewhat noteworthy, as you'll probably meet a character/NPC here, or just something that is visually for show.

Landmark levels are also shorter, but also clump with other level types, so you can expect to see a landmark level in the middle of a chase or puzzle level.

### Stealth Type
Stay out of sight, out of the light, and out of the way of enemies.

## Explaining the Roadmap and Level Layouts
One thing that is noteworthy as well is that the areas each have up to 100 levels. You'll notice that the levels are numbered in a way that makes sense. For example, Area 1 has levels 1-100, Area 2 has levels 101-200, and so on.

# Lincoln's Labyrinthine Intro Script
## First Frame
> Black screen. The sound of birds chirping, and the sound of a gentle breeze. 
"What does it take... to be crazy? To be a madman, to be a fool?"
## Second Frame
> A crow on the street, pecking at the ground on the sidewalk.
"To some, madness is a curse. A curse that makes you believe what's wrong has been right all along. To others, it's a blessing. A blessing that makes you see the world for what it is, and not what it should be."
> The crow looks up at the sky and sees a shadow appearing over him. His eyes bulge and teeth grit as he caws in terror. He then flies away.
## Third Frame
> Lincoln, walking up the street, his hands in his pockets, looking timid and depressed as usual. The sky is gray, and the wind is blowing. Lincoln is in his usual work clothes, and his hat looking like its weighing him down.
"This is the story of a man who was cursed with such madness. A man who's life was controlled by his inner self-deprecation."
## Fourth Frame
> Close up to Lincoln's face, his nose bobbing up and down as he walks. His eyes are looking down, and his mouth is closed.
"Lincoln, a bat, who couldn't keep his composure at the sight of dangers that lurk in every corner."
> Lincoln looks up. The camera pans to the left, and we see a neighbor cutting the hedges on their lawn. A cow bell rings on his neck.
## Fifth Frame
> The camera shows the cow person, who is a neighbor of Lincoln's, cutting the hedges on their lawn. She gives Lincoln a friendly wave.
## Sixth Frame
> A sinister stinger plays as it shows a close up of the hedge clippers. 
## Seventh Frame
> Lincoln, jolting in fear, as he sees the hedge clippers. He then looks down at the ground, back at the cow person, and back at the ground. He then tightens his coat and waves back at the cow person, who is still cutting the hedges. 
## Eighth Frame
> A perspective of the cow behind them, with Lincoln walking by in the distance. The cow person looks back towards the camera, scratching her head. She shrugs and then continues cutting the hedges. 
## Ninth Frame
> Lincoln, walking down the street again, his hands in his pockets, looking even more timid.
"Everywhere he went, he was met with the same fear. The same terror. The same dread. He couldn't escape it, no matter how hard he tried."
"Lincoln was timid. Haunted by his own fears and insecurities."
## Tenth Frame
> Lincoln continues walking down the street, nearly waddling at this point. The camera is towards the back of him, and we see him walk away from the camera. 
"What was he to do? He has no friends, no family, no one to turn to. He was alone in this world, and he knew it, too."
> Fade to Black. The sounds of the birds and the wind close. Then, the sound of a door closing.
## Eleventh Frame
> Footsteps play as the camera is zoomed in on a couch. Lincoln steps towards the couch, and sits down. 
"He felt safest in his own home. The one place where he could be himself, and not have to worry about the outside world."
## Twelfth Frame
> Lincoln, looks up, camera pointed towards his face from the bottom.
## Thirteenth Frame
> Camera closes up to his face. He looks to his left.
## Fourteenth Frame
> An image of his betrothed, Lasse, another bat whose fur is a lighter shade of brown than Lincoln's. He is wearing a suit, and his hair is slicked back. He is smiling, and his eyes are closed, and she was looking at him with a smile on her face. The camera is zoomed in on her face.
## Fifteenth Frame
> Lincoln shakes his head. 
Lincoln: "I can't do this. I can't be without her. This... home, this life, it's not enough. I need her. I need to be with her."
> Lincoln looks down at the ground, he looks sad, and his eyes are filled with tears.
## Sixteenth Frame
> A view of his shoes. He gets up, and walks to the right.
## Seventeenth Frame
> A view of the hallway. Lincoln slowly walks towards his bedroom, his ears bouncing up and down as he walks. The camera is static, and the sound of his footsteps echo in the hallway. 
## Eighteenth Frame
> Black frame. A door opens with a loud creak, and Lincoln stands there, looking inside.
## Nineteenth Frame
> A view of his bedroom. It pans to the right, and we see his bed. Beer bottles, food wrappers, clothes, and literal dust bunnies crumbling down can be found on the floor. He walks to the corner of the room.
## Twentieth Frame
> Lincoln, landed his back against the wall and sat down on the floor. He looks down at the ground, and his eyes are filled with tears. He picks up a beer bottle, and takes a swig. Empty. He frowns extremely, with a loud "Boowomp." His nose droops. He then angrily throws it against the wall.
## Twenty-First Frame
> Close up of Lincoln's face. He looks up, and his eyes are wide open. His pupils are dilated, and his ears are twitching. He looks around the room, and then looks forwards.
Lincoln: "What is happening to me?"
> His eyes, shaking, he looks down, and his ears droop. He closes his eyes, and flashes of his beloved Lasse appear in his mind. The music starts to pick up.
## Twenty-Second Frame
> Lincoln, squining his eyes, and his nose pulsating from his breathing. The music stops, and the sound of a door opening plays. His eyes are wide open. He looks around the room, and then looks forwards. The music starts to pick up again, and it's spooky.
"What?"
## Twenty-Third Frame
> Lincoln looks up. The camera shows a long hallway that is dark. Too dark to see clearly.
"It would seem that his home was not as safe as he thought."
> Fade to black.
"Will he be able to escape the horrors that await him? Or will he succumb to the madness that has been plaguing him for so long?"
> Lincoln then screams.
